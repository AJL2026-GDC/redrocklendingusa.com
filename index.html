Questions we hear most

I don't show much income on my tax returns. Can I still buy a rental?

Usually, yes. A DSCR loan qualifies on the property's rent rather than your personal income. It's the standard tool for investors who own businesses or write off heavily.

Can you help with land financing?

Yes, we arrange it through commercial and hard-money lending partners — typically land that's entitled or headed for construction.

What is "hard money," and when does it make sense?

Short-term, asset-based financing. It costs more than a bank loan but closes fast and looks at the property first. It fits purchases on a deadline, heavy renovations, and borrowers whose paperwork doesn't fit a bank's box — with a plan to refinance into long-term financing after.

Do you work with real estate agents?

All the time — including the agents at Goyal & DeCoster. Agents get a direct line and honest timelines.

Will you publish your rates?

No. Rates change daily and depend on the program, the property and the borrower, so a number on a website would be wrong by the time you read it. Terms come from the lending institution for your specific scenario.
