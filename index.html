Questions we hear most

I don't show much income on my tax returns. Can I still buy a rental?

Usually, yes. A DSCR loan qualifies on the property's rent rather than your personal income. It's the standard tool for investors who own businesses or write off heavily.

Do you finance land?

Yes, through our commercial and hard-money partners — typically land that's entitled or headed for construction. If you're buying a lot to build your own home, construction-to-permanent covers the land and the build in one closing.

What is "hard money," and when does it make sense?

Short-term, asset-based financing. It costs more than a bank loan but closes fast and looks at the property first. It fits purchases on a deadline, heavy renovations, and borrowers whose paperwork doesn't fit a bank's box — with a plan to refinance into long-term financing after.

I'm a foreign national. Can I get a mortgage in Florida?

Yes. Several programs lend to buyers whose income and assets are outside the United States. Expect a larger down payment and documentation of funds; we'll walk you through exactly what's needed.

Do you work with real estate agents?

All the time — including the agents at Goyal & DeCoster. Agents get a direct line, fast pre-qualifications and honest timelines.

Will you publish your rates?

No. Rates change daily and depend on the program, the property and the borrower, so a number on a website would be wrong by the time you read it. Call or write and we'll quote your scenario.
