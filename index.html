Why one lender matters

Red Rock Lending is part of the Red Rock Holdings group alongside Goyal & DeCoster Realty. The same philosophy runs through both: handle every real estate need a client has, so they never have to start over with a stranger.

  • Every product, one conversation

    Investment, commercial and residential teams rarely share an office. Here they share a desk — so a DSCR investor who also needs a primary-home refinance has one point of contact.

  • Financing that follows the deal

    Buying through Goyal & DeCoster? The lending side already knows the property, the timeline and the contract.

  • Plain answers on hard loans

    Construction and commercial financing are where most borrowers get lost. We explain the structure before you commit to anything.

  • Realtor friendly

    Agents get a direct line, fast pre-qualifications and honest timelines — the things that keep a contract alive.
